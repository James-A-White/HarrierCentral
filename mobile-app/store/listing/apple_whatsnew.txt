Harrier Central 3.1 - everything since 3.0.12, in one update.

Chat that reaches your phone: your kennel's chat, and a room for each role you hold, with notifications when someone posts.

Sign in without waiting for a code: use the passkey you made on hashruns.org, or point your camera at the QR code on your computer.

Bring your running history with you: import a GPX, TCX or FIT file, or a whole Strava or Garmin export, and your old runs join your run counts.

Your trails now live on your phone, so they draw instantly and work without a signal.

Find my photos picks a run's photos out of your camera roll, so the pack sees them without you hunting through thousands.

Past runs show what happened on them at a glance: the track, the photos, the chat and the down-downs, with counts.

Plus the fixes that matter most: the runs-list crash is gone, photos and admin marks no longer bend your recorded GPS trail, and check-in, payments and the pack list keep working on a bad signal.
