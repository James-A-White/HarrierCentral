Harrier Central 3.1.9 — everything since 3.1.8, in one update.

SAVE AND SEND
Kennel admins and hares can now email a run to the kennel as well as post it to WhatsApp. Write the email from the run's own description, or let AI draft it in your kennel's house style. Preview it to yourself first, and see exactly who will get it and why.

THE HARE'S OFFICIAL TRAIL
Hares can scout a trail before the run, or promote any runner's track to be the official trail. It is drawn on the PackTrack map, replays alongside the pack, and a lost runner who presses I'm Lost sees it on their map.

KENNEL TRAIL MAP
A Trail map button on the kennel page shows every official trail and every start point, with the kennel's favourite starts drawn bigger. Tap a pin to open any run that started there.

PACKTRACK
Tap Me to keep yourself in the middle of the map, and tap another hasher to see just the two of you and the trail between you. The radar now shows the streets behind it, zooming is smoother, and one button follows you on the map. About ten minutes before a run you said Yes to, PackTrack gets itself ready and arms auto start. Android trails are now as detailed as iPhone ones.

FIND A HASHER
Search for hashers by hash name or home kennel. You choose whether others can find you. Run Counts › By Hasher lists everyone you have run with, most runs together first.

CHATS
Chats now splits Groups from Friends, each with its own unread count. A chat bubble next to a hasher's name on lists across the app starts a conversation. The kennel page has its own kennel chat, and a run's chat is the bubble at the top of the run. Chat notifications are fewer and better targeted.

I WAS AT THIS RUN
On a past run, you can mark yourself as having been there, where your kennel allows it.

APPLE WATCH
A run that starts automatically now appears on your watch.

AND MORE
Runs imported from a kennel's website carry an AI badge. The runs date filter jumps to any year quickly. A phone with the wrong clock now corrects itself straight away. Fixes for a rare crash on iPhone, the Android navigation bar, chat badges after returning to the app, and auto start on Android.
