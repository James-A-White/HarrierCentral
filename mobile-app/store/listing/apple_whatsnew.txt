Harrier Central 3.1.8 — everything since 3.1.0, in one update.

MESSAGE ANOTHER HASHER
Long-press anyone's message and choose "Message" to start a private conversation. You decide who can message you — friends only (the default), anyone, or nobody — and a request waits at the top of Chats for you to accept or decline. Pin the conversations you care about, and see when your message has been read.

REPLY, REACT, SHARE
Reply to a message and your answer carries a quote of it. React with a thumbs up, a heart, a laugh, a beer, a runner or a flame. Send a photo, your location or a dropped pin, copy any message, and delete your own for everyone. Block someone and you will not see them in any chat; report a message and it goes to Harrier Central's reviewers.

CHATS, ONE TAP AWAY
The chat bubble opens Chats over whatever screen you are on, and the app icon shows how many messages you have not read.

AUTO START
Arm it before the run and put your phone away: your trail starts by itself when you set off — your phone notices you running, and never starts it because you drove off. Press Start yourself and nothing you ran before is lost.

THE SAME TRAIL ON EVERY SETTING
Every tracking setting now records the same detailed trail. The setting only decides how often your position is sent to the pack while your phone is in your pocket — sending is what uses the battery — and with the app open you are always up to date.

WHEN YOU NEED HELP
Send Help rings and buzzes the phones of the runners around you, even in a pocket. Before you start, the app checks the things that spoil a trail — location permission, battery saver — and a signal light on the run page shows how good your GPS is.

A PHONE WITH THE WRONG TIME
If your phone's clock is wrong, Harrier Central now corrects for it and keeps working, and tells you how to fix it.

PHOTOS ON THE MAP
Tap a photo on the map, then "Map" to see exactly where it was taken.
