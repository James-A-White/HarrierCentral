Harrier Central is the free app for Hash House Harriers everywhere. Find a run around the corner or across the globe, RSVP in a tap, and keep every run you have ever done stored safely in the cloud.

PACKTRACK — THE WHOLE PACK ON ONE LIVE MAP
See where everyone is, in real time. Drop trail marks as you go — checks, false trails, drink stops, or your kennel's own symbols. Find the pack with the radar, find your way back with the lost compass, and send an I'm Lost if the trail beats you. Snap photos and they land on the map where you took them. Replay any run afterwards, or share a link so friends and family can follow along in any browser.

TRAIL TV — PUT THE RUN ON THE BIG SCREEN
At the On-Inn, throw the run up on a TV: live tracks, a front-runner camera, photos arriving from trail, pack stats, and a QR code so anyone in the room can follow along on their own phone.

FOR EVERY HASHER
• A chat for every run, and one for every kennel you follow
• An interactive songbook — the songmeister picks a song and every phone switches to it
• Hash Flash: the run's photo gallery, with captions and cropping
• Hashes Around the World and an Explore map, for finding a hash when you travel
• Your full run history and stats, emailed to you as a spreadsheet whenever you want

FOR MISMANAGEMENT
• Check the pack in even with no signal — it syncs itself when you are back
• Take payments, charge memberships and sell haberdashery straight from check-in
• Manage runs, hares and attendance
• Set who can do what, kennel by kennel

Built by hashers, for hashers. Free, with no adverts.
