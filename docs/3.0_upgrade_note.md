## Full announcement

**Harrier Central 3.0 is here — the biggest update we've ever shipped.**

It's been almost a year since 2.1.2, and nearly every part of the app has been
rebuilt. Here's what's new.

### 🏃 PackTrack — the live trail, on everyone's phone

Start tracking from the Live Run screen and the whole pack shows up on one map,
live.

- **Trail marks from the trail.** Drop a Check, False Trail, Drink Stop, On Inn
  or a custom mark right from your phone (or your Apple Watch) and it lands on
  everyone's map instantly. Kennels can pick their own mark symbols.
- **"Where is everyone?"** — a radar view: you're the dot in the middle, every
  other hasher is a blip at their true bearing and distance.
- **"Where is the trail?"** — a compass that points you back to the pack's
  trail, counting down the distance as you walk, and tells you whose trail
  you're heading for. Works offline.
- **I'm Lost / Send Help** — a distress mark that goes to the pack immediately
  and shows on everyone's map until you're found.
- **Photos on the map.** Snap a photo mid-run and it pops out on the map where
  you took it. Multi Photo mode reopens the camera after every shot.
- **Replay any run.** Playback with speed control — tilt your phone to scrub —
  and admins can trim the official start and end afterwards.
- **Share the link.** Friends and family follow along live in any browser, no
  app needed.
- **Apple Watch companion** — distance, time and the trail-mark buttons on
  your wrist. **Android** now tracks in the background too.

### 📺 Trail TV — the run on the big screen

Every run now has a Trail TV page on hashruns.org, built for a TV at the On-Inn
or the pub: live tracks, a rolling carousel of the run's photos (a new photo
takes over the screen the moment it's approved — "FRESH FROM TRAIL"), mark
callouts, a stats ticker and a QR code so anyone in the room can follow along.
After the run it switches to replay mode with a front-runner camera. Share it
straight from the run's map in the app.

### 🎵 Interactive Songbook

Any RSVP'd hasher can share the current song with the whole pack in real time:
pick a song, tap **Share Now**, and everyone's phone switches to it instantly.
Open the songbook within a couple of minutes of a share and you land on the
active song automatically.

### ⚡ Much faster, much steadier

- The app now opens **straight to your runs** — no more waiting on the "Filling
  Your Mug" screen; data refreshes in the background.
- **Works offline.** Check-in, payments and the pack list all work without a
  signal and sync automatically when you get it back. The runs list never
  empties on a bad connection.
- Connectivity detection was rewritten from scratch: tunnels, dropped signal
  and slow servers are handled quietly instead of with error dialogs.
- Under the hood: a new state-management foundation, 14 legacy packages
  removed, lower memory and battery use on the live map, and proper crash
  reporting so we can fix what we can't reproduce.

### 💬 Better chat

- **Trail Chat** for every run and a **kennel chat** for every kennel you
  follow (beta).
- Unread counts on the run and kennel cards, an **Unseen Chats** list behind
  the chat badge, push notifications, and read state that syncs across your
  devices.

### 📸 Hash Flash — photos done properly

- Photo review with audiences — **Private / Members / Public** — plus a
  **Featured** flag and a **Cover** photo per run.
- Grid view with bulk **Delete / Keep Private / Share / Add to Gallery**.
- Non-destructive crop, captions, photographer credit, "View on map", and
  import from your camera roll (the photo lands on the map where it was
  taken, even in another time zone).

### 💷 Memberships, haberdashery and money

- Charge **memberships** from the check-in page — rolling, fixed-year or
  lifetime — and sell **haberdashery**, all without touching Hash Credit
  (though credit can pay).
- Membership + run fee in **one tap**; check-in warns when a membership is
  about to lapse.
- A payment report that shows every transaction on its own line, and payments
  queued offline that can't be lost or double-charged.

### 🌍 And more

- **Hashes Around the World** — browse runs anywhere without an account.
- **Explore map** of every run, past and future; a **Past Runs** section that's
  just yours.
- **Admin Portal** from the menu — the web portal, already logged in.
- Per-kennel permissions, a dedicated run-admin screen for hares, a rebuilt
  run editor with a single Save, and a Down Downs page.
- New onboarding with **Find My Account**, a Settings page, a Support page,
  web-friendly avatars — and Harrier Central is back on the **Play Store**.

Thank you to everyone who ran with the betas. On On!
