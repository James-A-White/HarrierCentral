## DESCRIPTION
---8<---
Harrier Central is the free app for Hash House Harriers everywhere. Find a run around the corner or across the globe, RSVP in a tap, and keep every run you have ever done stored safely in the cloud.

PackTrack puts the whole pack on one live map. Drop trail marks as you go, find everyone with the radar, find your way back with the lost compass, and snap photos that land on the map where you took them. Replay any run afterwards, or share a link so friends and family can follow along in any browser. Put it on the big screen at the On-Inn with Trail TV.

There is a chat for every run and every kennel you follow, an interactive songbook that switches the whole pack to the same song, and a Hash Flash gallery where the run's photos live.

For mismanagement there is plenty to make the job lighter: check the pack in even with no signal, take payments, charge memberships and sell haberdashery, manage runs and hares, and set who can do what for each kennel.

Built by hashers, for hashers.

On-On!

## WHAT'S NEW IN THIS VERSION
---8<---
Harrier Central 3.0 - our biggest update ever.

PackTrack: see the whole pack on one live map. Drop trail marks from your phone or Apple Watch, find everyone with the radar, find the trail with the lost compass, send an I'm Lost, snap photos onto the map, then replay or share any run.

Trail TV: put the run on a big screen at the On-Inn, with live tracks, photos, stats and a QR code so anyone in the room can follow along.

Now a native iPad app, full screen in both portrait and landscape.

Faster and steadier: opens straight to your runs, works offline for check-in, payments and the pack list, and copes quietly with a bad signal.

Better chat: Trail Chat for every run, kennel chat, unread badges and an Unseen Chats list.

Hash Flash: photo review with audiences, bulk actions, captions, crop and camera-roll import.

Memberships and haberdashery charged straight from check-in, with a clearer payment report.

Plus Hashes Around the World, an Explore map, the Admin Portal from the menu, per-kennel permissions and a new sign-up flow.
