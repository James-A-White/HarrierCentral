For Mismanagement

Simplify Managing Your Kennel

The perfect information management system for mismanagement.

  • Run counts, automated. The app keeps track for you — and even tells you when Hashers are reaching important milestones, with a special Drink category.
  • Hash Cash made easy. Recording is as simple as swiping left for cash or right for electronic transfer, with full transparency on what's collected at every trail.
  • Instant reports. Run stats and finance reports emailed with the press of a button — free.
Built Right

Beautiful Design, Secure Data

  • Modern by design. The iOS and Android apps follow Google's Material Design guidelines for the most intuitive interface possible — and we keep the app fresh as new interface techniques develop.
  • Private by default. Your data lives in the Microsoft Azure cloud and is never shared with third parties — not Facebook, not Google, nobody. Details of your Hash life stay right where they belong. Read our Privacy Policy.
  • Questions? Start with the FAQ, or drop us a line via the contact form. We'll reply once we've recovered from our latest Hashing adventure!
