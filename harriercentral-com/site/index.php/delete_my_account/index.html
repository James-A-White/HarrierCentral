How to Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go! You can request deletion of your Harrier Central account and its associated personal data at any time.

  1. Email us at connect@harriercentral.com from the email address registered to your Harrier Central account, with the subject “Delete My Account”. Include your Hash name if you have one — it helps us find the right account.
  2. We verify and delete. We'll confirm the request came from your registered address, then remove your account and personal information (name, email address, Hash name and profile details) from our systems.
  3. We confirm back to you by email once the deletion is complete.

Please note: anonymized, non-personal records (such as a Kennel's aggregate run history and Hash Cash totals) may be retained so that Kennel statistics remain correct for other members. Nothing that identifies you personally is kept. Questions? See our Privacy Policy or use the contact form.
