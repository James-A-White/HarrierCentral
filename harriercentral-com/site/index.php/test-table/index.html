TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Harrier Central is a part-time project from a pair of enthusiastic Hashers. We don't make any money from this project and are doing it to support Hashers and Hash Kennels around the world. Your Kennel may use our services and app on the conditions that

  1. you offer Tuna Melt and Opee each one free run per year with your Kennel if we drop in,
  2. you also offer to help us find crash space if we are visiting your area, and
  3. someone from your Kennel joins us at your favorite local Hash hangout on a non-Hash night.

Finally, we have to charge for some features to cover the cost of secure cloud hosting and to offset some of our software development costs. With this in mind we plan to keep the app free forever for Hashers and and only charge kennels a fair and reasonable fee to use advanced features; we are not trying to profit from the Hash.
