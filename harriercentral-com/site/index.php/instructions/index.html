Everyday How-Tos

Following a Kennel

When you Follow a Kennel, you are sure to see their next runs on your Runs List without having to search for them, regardless of your location. You can Follow as many or as few Kennels as you want.

  1. Click on the house icon at the bottom of the screen to go to the Kennels screen.
  2. Use the search bar at the top of the Kennels screen to find the desired Kennel.
  3. Click on the box to the left of the Kennel's name.
  4. Choose if you always want to see the runs, or just runs within your default distance.
  5. You can also set it as your Home Kennel — keep in mind you can only have one Home Kennel.

Checking in for a Trail

Once you are within one hour and one mile (1.6 km) of a run start posted on Harrier Central, open the app and a dialog will pop up asking if you'd like to Check-in. This way you can track your run counts even if your Kennel isn't using all of the HC Admin Tools.
