Add your Kennel to Harrier Central

with automatic synchronization of events from your Facebook Group

It’s easy to add your Kennel to Harrier Central and configure it for integration with your Facebook Group. Please note, Facebook Integration does not with Facebook Pages or groups that are hidden (only public or visible private groups); if this applies to your kennel, you can continue with this form or switch to the No Integration Form. To start, the first step is to fill out this online form that will provide us enough information to get your Kennel configured in Harrier Central. The second step is to login to Facebook using either the Harrier Central App (preferred method) or using our Web portal. And the third and final step is to add the Harrier Central App to your Facebook Group.

Start by filling out this form, and once you are done with that, you will be directed to another page that walks you through steps #2 and #3. If you have any questions, you can use our contact form and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Harrier Central is a part-time project from a pair of enthusiastic Hashers. We don't make any money from this project and are doing it to support Hashers and Hash Kennels around the world. Your Kennel may use our services and app on the conditions that

  1. you offer Tuna Melt and Opee each one free run per year with your Kennel if we drop in,
  2. you also offer to help us find crash space if we are visiting your area, and
  3. someone from your Kennel joins us at your favorite local Hash hangout on a non-Hash night.

Finally, we have to charge for some features to cover the cost of secure cloud hosting and to offset some of our software development costs. With this in mind we plan to keep the app free forever for Hashers and and only charge kennels a fair and reasonable fee to use advanced features; we are not trying to profit from the Hash.
