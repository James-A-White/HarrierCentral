The Fine Print

Terms and Conditions

Your Kennel may use our services and app on the conditions that:

  1. You offer Tuna Melt and Opee each one free run per year with your Kennel if we drop in,
  2. You also offer to help us find crash space if we are visiting your area, and
  3. Someone from your Kennel joins us at your favorite local Hash hangout on a non-Hash night.

We have to charge for some features to cover the cost of secure cloud hosting and to offset some of our software development costs. We plan to keep the app free forever for Hashers and only charge Kennels a fair and reasonable fee for advanced features — we are not trying to profit from the Hash.

OnOn! — Tuna Melt and Opee
